Good Day Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

Good Day is a habit tracker made by Jacob Saenger. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. I don't collect your data, and I can't see it.

What the app stores

Everything you enter stays with you:

  • your habits: names, icons, colors, schedules, targets and reminder times
  • your check-ins, including amounts and any journal notes
  • your settings

This data is stored on your iPhone, where Good Day's widgets can show it.

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, Good Day keeps your data in your private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit, so it shows up on your other devices and your Apple Watch. That database belongs to your Apple Account: I have no access to it. Apple's Privacy Policy covers iCloud. You can turn off iCloud for Good Day in your iPhone's iCloud settings.

Purchases

Good Day Pro is sold through the App Store, and Apple handles the payment. I don't receive your name or payment details; the app only learns whether you have Pro.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. Good Day has no server that sends notifications.

Siri and Shortcuts

Logging a habit with Siri or Shortcuts runs on your device through Apple's frameworks. Apple's privacy terms cover Siri.

Sharing and export

Exporting your data or sharing Good Day with a friend uses the iOS share sheet. Nothing is sent anywhere except where you choose to send it.

No third parties

Good Day contains no third-party code for analytics, advertising or crash reporting. If you've chosen in iOS settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with me; you can turn this off in Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Good Day doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

You can delete any habit in the app. Deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone. Data kept in iCloud can be removed from your iPhone's iCloud storage settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated with a new effective date.
