Good Day Support

Help with the Good Day habit tracker

Questions, problems or ideas? Email jacob.w.saenger@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Restore a purchase

In Good Day, tap the ••• button, then Settings, and tap Restore Purchases in the Good Day Pro section. Use the same Apple Account you bought Pro with. Restore Purchases is also on the Good Day Pro screen.

Cancel a subscription

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Good Day. While you're subscribed, Good Day's Settings also has a Manage Subscription button. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

Bought Lifetime while subscribed?

Apple doesn't cancel a subscription when you buy Lifetime. Cancel the subscription as above so you're not charged again; you keep Pro forever.

Syncing between devices

Good Day syncs through your private iCloud. Make sure you're signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on each device, and that iCloud is turned on for Good Day. The Apple Watch app gets your habits the same way.

Widgets

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets are part of Good Day Pro. Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Good Day.

Export or delete your data

With Good Day Pro, tap ••• and then Export Data to get your check-ins as a CSV file. You can delete any habit and its history from the app, or archive it to hide it without losing its history.

Privacy

Good Day has no account, ads or tracking. Read the privacy policy.
