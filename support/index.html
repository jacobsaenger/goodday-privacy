Help with the Good Day habit tracker

Questions, problems or ideas? Email jacob.w.saenger@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Syncing between devices

Good Day syncs through your private iCloud. Make sure you're signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on each device, and that iCloud is turned on for Good Day. The Apple Watch app gets your habits the same way, and its complication also gets them straight from your iPhone.

Widgets

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Good Day. Widgets show your habits as suns over a horizon and let you check in with one tap. On iOS 18 or later you can also add a Check In control to Control Center. Touch and hold the Good Day icon to check in a habit or start a new one.

Habits on certain days, habits to break, and pausing

In a habit's editor, choose On Certain Days under Goal to pick its weekdays: the other days never break its streak. Choose Break a Habit when you make it for something to stop, like "No sugar": every day without a slip counts, and you log a slip from Home or the habit's page. To pause a habit for a trip or an illness, turn on Pause This Habit: paused days don't break its streak and there are no reminders. Resume it from its page at any time.

Apple Health

A habit that just checks in can be checked in by Apple Health. Open the habit, tap the pencil, and under Apple Health choose a measure (steps, sleep, a workout, exercise or mindful minutes, water, or walking and running distance) and a daily target. Good Day then logs the days you reach it, on your iPhone and your Apple Watch. If nothing gets checked in, open the Health app, tap your picture, then Apps, and allow Good Day to read that measure. A day you remove stays removed.

Locking the app

Tap the gear at the top of Home to open Settings, and turn on Lock Good Day. Your iPhone then asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode each time you open the app. On a locked iPhone, widgets hide your habits, and checking in from a widget, Control Center, Siri or a reminder asks you to unlock first.

Importing your history

Open Settings (the gear), tap Import Data and choose a CSV file: one made by Export Data, or any file with a column for the habit and a column for the date. Good Day shows what the file would add before adding it, and never changes a day you have already logged.

Export or delete your data

Open Settings (the gear) and tap Export Data to get a CSV file with your habits, their settings, your check-ins and your journal notes. Importing it brings them back. You can delete any habit and its history from the app, or archive it to hide it without losing its history.

Good Day Unlimited

Good Day keeps up to 3 habits with every feature. Good Day Unlimited, a one-time purchase, unlocks as many as you like on all your devices, and with Family Sharing. Already bought it on another device? Open Settings (the gear) and tap Restore Purchase.

iPad and Mac

Good Day runs on iPad, and on Macs with Apple silicon from the Mac App Store. Your habits sync through iCloud.

Privacy

Good Day has no account, ads or tracking. Read the privacy policy.
